Privacy

What we collect, why, and what we don't do.

Last updated October 18, 2026.

Email alerts

If you sign up for alerts, we keep your email address so we can tell you when silver moves to Favorable on our buy scale. That is the only thing we use it for. We don't sell, rent or share your address with anyone.

Sign-ups and emails are handled by Buttondown, an email service, which stores your address on our behalf. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from future alerts right away.

Visitor counts

We count visits with GoatCounter, which records page views, the referring site, and general browser, screen size and country information. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across other websites, and doesn't collect personal information.

Other services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and the chart library from cdnjs. These services receive your IP address when your browser downloads those files, as with any website. News headlines link to the original publishers, whose own privacy policies apply once you visit their sites.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
